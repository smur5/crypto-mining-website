1. General Risk Warning

Cryptocurrency and digital asset activities involve significant financial and technological risks. The value of digital assets can change rapidly and may result in partial or complete loss of funds.

You should carefully evaluate your financial circumstances, objectives, and risk tolerance before participating in any cryptocurrency-related activity.

2. Cryptocurrency Market Risk

Cryptocurrency markets can be highly volatile. Prices may increase or decrease substantially within short periods of time.

Historical performance does not guarantee future results. Any prices, estimates, examples, or projections displayed on this website are provided for informational purposes only.

3. Mining Risk

Cryptocurrency mining depends on several factors, including network difficulty, cryptocurrency prices, electricity costs, hardware performance, maintenance, network conditions, and other operational expenses.

Mining activity may not always generate a profit. Returns can change over time and may become lower than expected.

4. No Guaranteed Returns

Blockchain networks, cryptocurrency protocols, mining equipment, internet services, software, wallets, and third-party services may experience interruptions, failures, security incidents, or unexpected changes.

Such events may affect the availability, performance, or value of digital assets.

6. Regulatory Risk

Cryptocurrency regulations vary between countries and may change over time. Changes in laws, regulations, taxation, licensing requirements, or government policies may affect digital asset activities.

You are responsible for understanding and complying with the laws and regulations applicable to you.

7. Security Risk

Digital assets and online accounts may be exposed to cybersecurity threats, including phishing, unauthorized access, malware, account compromise, fraudulent activity, and other attacks.

You should use strong passwords, protect your account credentials, and never share sensitive authentication information with another person.

8. Third-Party Risk

Certain services may depend on third-party providers, networks, infrastructure, payment providers, blockchain networks, or external platforms.

We cannot guarantee the availability, reliability, security, or performance of third-party services.

You are responsible for conducting your own research and making your own decisions before participating in cryptocurrency or mining activities.
